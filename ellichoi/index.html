Elli Choi

Stage. Performance. Emotion.

Stage. Performance. Emotion.

Having performed on the world's stages from a young age, award-winning violinist Elli Choi is rapidly establishing herself as one of the most captivating violinists of her generation. Most recently, she was awarded the Silver Medal at the 2026 International Violin Competition of Indianapolis.

Choi has performed throughout North America, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East at esteemed venues such as Carnegie Hall, David Geffen Hall and David H. Koch Theater, the Berliner Philharmonie, Kimmel Center's Verizon Hall, Maison Symphonique de Montréal, Tonhalle Zürich, Rudolfinum Dvořák Hall in Prague, BOZAR Brussels, the Abu Dhabi Palace, and Beijing's National Center for the Performing Arts, appearing with major orchestras such as the Orchestre Philarmonique de Monte-Carlo, the Belgian National Orchestra, Orquestra Sinfónica do Porto Casa da Música, the Novosibirsk Philharmonic Orchestra, Sofia Philharmonic Orchestra, Ulster Orchestra, Orchestre Philharmonique Royal de Liège, the Armenian State Symphony Orchestra, and The Juilliard Orchestra. In the 2026/27 season, she joins the Romanian Symphony Orchestra and conductor Gabriel Bebeșelea for a European tour, performing Paganini's First Violin Concerto in halls including the Kölner Philharmonie, the Wiener Musikverein, and the Concertgebouw Amsterdam.

Elli has been invited to perform for several heads of state, including President Emmanuel Macron of France and former Vice President Mike Pence at the United States Department of State, as well as the Duchess of Gloucester at Buckingham Palace. In July 2026, she appeared with the Belgian National Orchestra at the National Day concert in Brussels, in the presence of the King and Queen of the Belgians. She has also enjoyed performing at distinguished events, most notably the 2019 Lincoln Center Fall Gala honoring John E. Waldron, the 2022 KIND gala "Coming Together for Children Alone" held at the Museum of Natural History in New York City, the annual American Chamber of Commerce EU gala in Brussels, and at the World Children's Day Concert at the Dubai EXPO 2020 as a UNICEF ambassador.

Winner of the Monaco World Music Masters in 2016, Choi has since won top prizes at the Qingdao International Violin Competition, the Classic Strings International Competition held in Dubai, and the China International Music Competition, and took First Prize at the 2024 Dubai Classic Violin Competition. She was awarded Third Prize at the 2024 Queen Elisabeth Violin Competition, and in 2026 the Silver Medal in Indianapolis.

Elli completed her undergraduate studies at Columbia University while simultaneously enrolled at the Juilliard School, where she has studied since 2009 and earned her Master's degree as a Kovner Fellow. She continues her studies at Kronberg Academy with Professor Antje Weithaas as part of the Professional Studies under the patronage of the Ginkgo Foundation.

Choi currently plays the 1686 Antonio Stradivari, on generous loan from Jonathan Solars Fine Violins.
