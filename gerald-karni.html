Biography

For the 2026/27 season, Gerald Karni marks his second year in the role of Conducting Fellow with the San Diego Symphony Orchestra under the guidance of Music Director Rafael Payare. Raised and educated across Europe and the United States, his international career has rapidly gained momentum through performances and collaborations with leading orchestras across Europe and North America.

A significant milestone in his career was his collaboration with the New York Philharmonic, where, while serving as Assistant Conductor, he led the world premiere of Melinda Wagner's Elegy Flywheel at New York's Merkin Hall. Karni is a three-time recipient of the prestigious Career Assistance Award from the Solti Foundation U.S. and was a Conducting Fellow at the Verbier Festival.

Highlights of the current season include major international projects, including debuts with the Nuremberg Symphony Orchestra, the Tucson Symphony Orchestra, the Brașov Philharmonic, and the MÁV Symphony Orchestra Budapest. In spring 2027, he will continue his collaboration with the San Diego Symphony by assisting Music Director Rafael Payare and the orchestra on their upcoming European tour.

His profile as a conductor and sought-after collaborator on the podium has been further shaped through close collaborations with leading orchestras, including the Baltimore Symphony Orchestra, the San Diego Symphony, and the Rundfunk-Sinfonieorchester Berlin, through assistant conducting roles.

As a Conducting Fellow at the Verbier Festival in 2022 and 2023, he assisted Sir Simon Rattle, Gábor Takács-Nagy, Gianandrea Noseda, and Klaus Mäkelä. He has since conducted orchestras including the NDR Elbphilharmonie Orchestra, the Lucerne Symphony Orchestra, the Hamburger Camerata at the Elbphilharmonie, the Orchestra della Svizzera Italiana, and the Sønderjyllands Symfoniorkester. In May 2024, he stepped in at short notice to lead a concert week with the Belgrade Philharmonic Orchestra, conducting Strauss's Don Quixote with Daniel Müller-Schott, Debussy's Ibéria, and Márquez's Danzón No. 2. Additional engagements have taken him to the Haifa Symphony Orchestra, the Mozartfest Augsburg, and the Traunsteiner Sommerkonzerte, where he collaborated with soloists Vilde Frang and Maximilian Hornung.

Karni began his musical training as a violist, studying with Lawrence Power at the Zurich University of the Arts (ZHdK), before completing his Master's degree in Orchestral Conducting with Marc Kissóczy at the Conservatorio della Svizzera Italiana. While still a student, he won the Silver Medal at the 3rd International Antal Doráti Conducting Competition in Budapest, marking an early breakthrough on the international podium. During his time in Zurich, he founded the Side-by-Side initiative, bringing together musicians from the Tonhalle Orchestra Zurich and the Zurich Opera Orchestra with students. Since 2015, he has also been a member of the West-Eastern Divan Orchestra, where his longstanding collaboration with its founder, Daniel Barenboim, has played a formative role in his artistic development.
