PREDAN VOIGT PRODUCTIONS

International orchestra tours
in collaboration with TVM artists & PHAM

Predan Voigt Productions, founded in Berlin in 2012 by Thomas Voigt and Dacian Predan-Hallabrin, specializes in the global management, logistics, and strategic execution of high-profile international orchestra tours. The company books top-tier ensembles into the world’s most prestigious venues and manages artistic careers.

The agency’s international network includes renowned symphony and chamber orchestras such as the Konzerthausorchester Berlin, the Rundfunk-Sinfonieorchester Berlin, the Dresden Philharmonic, the Philharmonia Orchestra London, the Hong Kong Philharmonic Orchestra, the SWR Symphonieorchester Stuttgart, the Deutsche Radiophilharmonie Saarbrücken, the MDR-Sinfonieorchester Leipzig, the Prague Philharmonia, the Berlin Chamber Orchestra, the Orquesta Sinfónica Nacional de México, the Romanian National Symphony Orchestra, and the Trondheim Soloists.

Furthermore, the agency regularly represents leading early music formations and specialized ensembles, including the Akademie für Alte Musik Berlin, Les Siècles, L’Arpeggiata, Jordi Savall and his ensembles, the 12 Cellists of the Berlin Philharmonic, German Brass, and the Netherlands Blazers Ensemble.
