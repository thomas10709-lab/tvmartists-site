International Orchestra Tours

Predan Voigt Pro­duc­tions, founded in Berlin in 2012 by Thomas Voigt and Dacian Predan-Hal­labrin, spe­cial­izes in the global man­age­ment, logis­tics, and strate­gic exe­cu­tion of high-profile inter­na­tional orches­tra tours. The company books top-tier ensem­bles into the world’s most pres­ti­gious venues and manages artis­tic careers.

The agen­cy’s inter­na­tional network includes renowned sym­phony and chamber orches­tras such as the Berlin Konzert­haus Orches­tra, the Berlin Radio Sym­phony Orches­tra, the Dresden Phil­har­monic, the Phil­har­mo­nia Orches­tra London, the Hong Kong Phil­har­monic Orches­tra, the SWR Sym­pho­nie­or­ches­ter Stutt­gart, the Deutsche Radio­phil­har­mo­nie Saar­brü­cken, the MDR-Sin­fo­nie­or­ches­ter Leipzig, the Prague Phil­har­mo­nia, the Berlin Chamber Orches­tra, the Orquesta Sin­fó­nica Nacio­nal de México, the Roma­nian National Sym­phony Orches­tra, and the Trond­heim Soloists.

Fur­ther­more, the agency reg­u­larly rep­re­sents leading early music for­ma­tions and spe­cial­ized ensem­bles, includ­ing the Aka­de­mie für Alte Musik Berlin, Les Siècles, L’Arpeg­giata, Jordi Savall and his ensem­bles, the 12 Cel­lists of the Berlin Phil­har­monic, German Brass, and the Nether­lands Blazers Ensem­ble.
