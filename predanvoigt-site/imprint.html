Privacy Policy for predanvoigt.com

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit this website.

1. Personal Information We Collect

When you visit predanvoigt.com, we may automatically collect certain information about your device, including your web browser, IP address, time zone, and some cookies installed on your device.

If you contact us through a form or by email, we may also collect your name, email address, and any message you submit.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Analyze website usage to enhance user experience

3. Cookies

We use cookies to collect information about how visitors interact with our website. Cookies are small data files stored on your device.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features of the website may not function properly.

4. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with the website. Google Analytics collects anonymous information such as pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring websites.

This data helps us improve the website, but it does not identify individual users. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except as necessary to comply with laws or protect rights.

6. Your Rights

If you are a resident of the EU or other jurisdictions with data protection laws, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Please contact us to make such a request.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary for the purposes stated in this policy.
