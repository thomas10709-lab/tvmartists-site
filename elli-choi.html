Elli Choi

Prize Winner of
Indianapolis Violin Competition 2026
Queen Elisabeth Competition 2024
Dubai Violin Competition 2024
Videos Photo Download Official Website

In her mid-twenties, award-winning violinist Elli Choi has already established herself as one of the most talented violinists of her generation.

Elli has performed with leading orchestras including the Orchestre Philharmonique de Monte-Carlo, the Belgian National Orchestra, the Ulster Orchestra, the Indianapolis Symphony Orchestra, the Orchestra dell’Accademia Teatro alla Scala, the Orchestre Philharmonique Royal de Liège, the Novosibirsk Philharmonic Orchestra, the Sofia Philharmonic Orchestra, the Salzburg Chamber Soloists, the Camerata Bern, the Armenian State Orchestra, and the Juilliard Orchestra, working with conductors such as Massimo Zanetti, Hugh Wolff, Leonard Slatkin, Gabriel Bebeșelea, Anthony Hermus, and Nayden Todorov.

Her career has taken her across North America, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East, to venues including David Geffen Hall and the David H. Koch Theater at Lincoln Center, the Berliner Philharmonie, the Concertgebouw Amsterdam, Verizon Hall at the Kimmel Center, the Maison Symphonique de Montréal, the Tonhalle Zürich, the Dvořák Hall at the Rudolfinum in Prague, BOZAR in Brussels, the Emirates Palace in Abu Dhabi, and the National Center for the Performing Arts in Beijing.

Recent and upcoming highlights include her debuts at the Concertgebouw Amsterdam with the Ulster Orchestra, performing Bruch’s Violin Concerto; at the Casa da Música in Porto with the Orquestra Sinfónica do Porto Casa da Música, performing Brahms’ Violin Concerto; and at the Auditorio de Zaragoza with the Orquestra Reino de Aragón, performing Vivaldi’s The Four Seasons. She also performed Bruch’s Violin Concerto with the Belgian National Orchestra in celebration of Belgium’s National Day.

In the 2026/27 season, Elli Choi returns to the Concertgebouw Amsterdam as part of a European tour with the Romanian Symphony Orchestra under Gabriel Bebeșelea, and makes her debut at Vienna’s Musikverein.

In 2026, she won the Silver Medal at the International Violin Competition of Indianapolis, and in 2024 she was awarded Third Prize at the Queen Elisabeth Competition in Brussels. She has also won First Prize at the China International Music Competition (2024), the Dubai Violin Competition (2024), and the Qingdao International Violin Competition. Her early success includes winning the Monaco World Music Masters in 2016.

Elli Choi has been invited to perform for several heads of state, including French President Emmanuel Macron and, at the U.S. Department of State, former Vice President Mike Pence, as well as the Duchess of Gloucester at Buckingham Palace. Her performances at special events include the 2019 Lincoln Center Fall Gala honoring John E. Waldron, the 2022 KIND gala “Coming Together for Children Alone” at the American Museum of Natural History in New York, the annual American Chamber of Commerce EU gala in Brussels, and the World Children’s Day Concert at Dubai Expo 2020, where she appeared as a UNICEF ambassador.

Elli studied at the Juilliard School from 2009 and completed her Master’s degree in Violin there in 2024 as a Kovner Fellow under Li Lin. In parallel, she earned a Bachelor’s degree in Economics and History from Columbia University. She is currently enrolled in the Professional Studies program at the Kronberg Academy, where she studies with Antje Weithaas and Boris Garlitsky, supported by the Gingko Foundation and J&A Beare’s.

She currently plays a J. B. Vuillaume violin, c. 1840, on generous loan.
