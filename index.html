Orchestra Touring

Our international network proudly includes some of the world's most renowned symphony and chamber orchestras. We are privileged to collaborate with esteemed ensembles such as the Konzerthausorchester Berlin, the Rundfunk-Sinfonieorchester Berlin, the Dresden Philharmonic, the Philharmonia Orchestra London, the Hong Kong Philharmonic Orchestra, the KBS Symphony Orchestra and the SWR Symphonieorchester Stuttgart. Our roster also features the Deutsche Radiophilharmonie Saarbrücken, the MDR-Sinfonieorchester Leipzig, the Prague Philharmonia, the Berlin Chamber Orchestra, the Orquesta Sinfónica Nacional de México, the Romanian National Symphony Orchestra, and the Trondheim Soloists. Furthermore, we regularly represent leading early music formations and highly specialized ensembles that are redefining their genres. This stellar lineup includes the Akademie für Alte Musik Berlin, Les Siècles, L'Arpeggiata, Jordi Savall and his ensembles, the 12 Cellists of the Berlin Philharmonic, German Brass, and the Netherlands Blazers Ensemble.

About & Contact


TVM artists was established in 2026 by Thomas Voigt, who has been active in the international cultural management sector since 1995 and since 1998, he has also served as the General Manager of conductor Michael Sanderling. The agency's core activities include the strategic development of artist careers, the acquisition of international tours for symphony orchestras, and advisory services for cultural institutions.


Thomas Voigt, born and raised in Germany, comes from a family of musicians. He studied violin and piano in Leipzig and Berlin and served as a violinist with the Berlin Radio Symphony Orchestra from 1990 to 1997. From 1989 to 2014, he was general manager of the Berlin Chamber Orchestra, leading the ensemble on extensive tours throughout Europe, Asia, and the Americas. In this role, he also organized his own subscription concert series featuring a wide variety of concerts at the Berlin Philharmonie and the Konzerthaus Berlin. Since 1995, he has been working as an artist agent, concert promoter and consultant, collaborating with leading orchestras and artists.
